# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Lemma to be a place where students, teachers, and contributors can work without harassment, regardless of age, experience, background, identity, or ability. We pledge to make participation respectful and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and kind. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, especially with students and first-time contributors.
- Respect originality and attribution. Do not submit material you do not have the right to use.
- Accept correction gracefully. A wrong answer is a normal part of mathematics.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Plagiarising problems, solutions, or code.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any project space where someone is representing Lemma.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method on their GitHub profile. If a private channel is not available, use GitHub's report feature. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may:

1. Ask for a change in behaviour.
2. Remove or edit content.
3. Close a thread or pull request.
4. Temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Decisions should be proportionate and explained to the people involved where practical.
